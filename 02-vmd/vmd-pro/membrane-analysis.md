# Membrane Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze lipid bilayers, membrane proteins, and other membrane-containing molecular systems.

## Planned Topics

- Selecting lipid species and membrane leaflets
- Identifying the membrane normal
- Bilayer thickness and area-related measurements
- Lipid order parameters
- Density profiles along the membrane normal
- Water or ion penetration
- Contacts between proteins and lipids
- Periodic boundary condition considerations

## Suggested Outcome

Create a membrane-specific analysis workflow with clearly documented leaflet and lipid selections.
