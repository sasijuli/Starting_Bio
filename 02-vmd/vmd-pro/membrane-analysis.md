# Membrane Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze lipid bilayers, membrane proteins, and other membrane-containing molecular systems.

## Planned Topics

- Selecting lipid species and membrane leaflets
- Identifying the membrane normal
- Bilayer thickness and area-related measurements
- Lipid order parameters
- Density profiles along the membrane normal
- Water or ion penetration
- Contacts between proteins and lipids
- Periodic boundary condition considerations

## Suggested Outcome

Create a membrane-specific analysis workflow with clearly documented leaflet and lipid selections.

## Supplementary Resource

The [Designing Molecular Membrane Models With VMD](https://phys.libretexts.org/Courses/University_of_California_Davis/Biophysics_241%3A_Membrane_Biology/07%3A_Computational_Characterization_of_Membranes/7.04%3A_Designing_Molecular_Membranes_Models_with_VMD) tutorial connects VMD visualization with membrane model preparation and introduces the use of CHARMM-GUI Membrane Builder.

Use it after completing VMD Basics and before developing system-specific membrane analyses. See the local [membrane resource summary](../link_summaries.md#membrane-modeling-and-analysis).
