# Restarts And Troubleshooting

## Purpose

Restart interrupted simulations and diagnose common failures without losing track of simulation state.

## Planned Topics

- Coordinate, velocity, and extended-system restart files
- Restart configuration patterns
- Step numbering and output naming
- Reading warnings and fatal errors
- Unstable temperature, pressure, or energy
- Bad contacts and failed minimization
- Periodic cell problems
- Missing or incompatible parameters
- Preserving logs and failed inputs for diagnosis

## Suggested Outcome

Create a restart checklist that confirms all restart files come from the same saved step.
