# Membrane Simulations

## Purpose

Configure and validate molecular dynamics simulations containing lipid bilayers or membrane proteins.

## Planned Topics

- Membrane system preparation
- Lipid and protein parameter compatibility
- Periodic cell dimensions and membrane orientation
- Pressure control and flexible-cell settings
- Restraints during equilibration
- Water and ion placement
- Monitoring membrane stability
- Preparing outputs for VMD membrane analysis

## Suggested Outcome

Build a staged equilibration plan that preserves membrane integrity while allowing the system to relax.
