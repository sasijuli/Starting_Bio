# Protein-Ligand Analysis

## Purpose

Study interactions between a protein and a bound ligand across a structure or trajectory.

## Planned Topics

- Defining stable protein and ligand selections
- Measuring binding-site distances
- Tracking contacts and hydrogen bonds
- Identifying nearby residues
- Monitoring ligand orientation and movement
- Aligning trajectories to the binding site
- Comparing interaction patterns across time
- Avoiding selection errors caused by residue naming

## Suggested Outcome

Produce a table of persistent protein-ligand contacts and a visual representation of the binding site.
