# Equilibration And Production

## Purpose

Design a staged workflow that moves a prepared system from minimization to stable production dynamics.

## Planned Topics

- Energy minimization
- Heating schedules
- Positional restraints and gradual release
- NVT and NPT ensembles
- Thermostat and barostat choices
- Timestep and rigid-bond settings
- Output frequencies
- Transition from equilibration to production
- Stability and convergence checks

## Suggested Outcome

Create separate, documented configuration files for minimization, equilibration stages, and production.
