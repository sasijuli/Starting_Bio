# Advanced Trajectory Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze structural changes across a molecular dynamics trajectory with reproducible selections and frame handling.

## Planned Topics

- Periodic boundary condition corrections
- Alignment and removal of global translation and rotation
- RMSD and RMSF
- Radius of gyration
- Distances, contacts, and hydrogen bonds over time
- Secondary-structure changes
- Time-series output and plotting preparation
- Frame stride, equilibration removal, and convergence checks

## Suggested Outcome

Build a documented workflow that prepares a trajectory, performs at least two complementary analyses, and exports results for plotting.
